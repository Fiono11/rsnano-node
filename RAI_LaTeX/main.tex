\title{RAI: Safe Pipelined Epoch Transitions\\for Account-Local Payments}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous Author(s)}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Regular paper}}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Single-writer payments finalize from account-local quorums without a global transaction sequence. Replacing validators is the hard part: votes already released by the outgoing committee can still form a final certificate after the handoff. RAI lets the next epoch's account voting proceed while the previous epoch closes. Each outgoing validator freezes one signed report; a deterministic construction turns an eligible selection of reports into a checkpoint carrying finality and origin-stamped locks. RAI is safe and terminates given the safety and termination of abstract reconstruction and agreement services. A predecessor gate, a prefix-wide overlap exception, late notarization, and a no-fast-path rule for early votes keep the pipeline safe; TLA\textsuperscript{+} model checking exposes three earlier rules' failures and verifies the repairs. A prototype in RsNano settles nine fork and fault workloads at 2,000 blocks/s, with median latency of 116 to 131\,ms without forks, and stays consistent across restarts and committee changes.
\end{abstract}
