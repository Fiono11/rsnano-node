\title{RAI: Safe Pipelined Epoch Transitions\\for Account-Local Payments}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous Author(s)}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Regular paper}}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Single-writer payments finalize from account-local quorums without a global transaction sequence. Replacing the validators that issue those quorums is the hard part: votes already released by the outgoing committee can still form a final certificate after the handoff. RAI is an epoch-transition protocol that lets the next epoch's account voting proceed while the previous epoch closes. Each outgoing validator freezes one signed report; RAI defines which report selections are eligible checkpoint inputs and a deterministic construction that turns them into a checkpoint carrying finality and origin-stamped locks. Reconstruction and agreement are abstract services, and RAI is safe and terminates given their safety and termination. A predecessor gate, a prefix-wide overlap exception, late notarization, and a no-fast-path rule for early votes keep the pipeline safe; TLA\textsuperscript{+} model checking exhibits the failures of three earlier rules and verifies the repaired ones. A prototype in RsNano settles nine fork and fault workloads at an offered 2,000 blocks/s with median latency near 100\,ms.
\end{abstract}
