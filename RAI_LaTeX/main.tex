\title{RAI: Safe Payments Through Committee Changes\\Without Stopping}
\author{\IEEEauthorblockN{Anonymous Author(s)}
\IEEEauthorblockA{Regular paper}}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Payment systems such as Nano and FastPay confirm each account's transactions separately, with no global order, in one network round trip. Two things remain hard. When the validators change, votes already given by the old validators can still complete a confirmation after the handover, so a careless handover can confirm two conflicting payments. And when an account signs two conflicting payments, by mistake or on purpose, most systems leave it stuck forever. RAI adds epochs to such a system. Each epoch ends in a checkpoint built from signed reports of the outgoing validators, and the next epoch votes while the previous one closes, under a few rules that keep the two from contradicting each other. Checkpoints also give every conflicting payment a decision. We prove safety and termination, model-check the handover rules, and show on a prototype that transitions cost little latency where a stop-and-drain handover stalls confirmations.
\end{abstract}
