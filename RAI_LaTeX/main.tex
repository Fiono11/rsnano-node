\begin{abstract}
FastPay and Nano confirm each account's payments separately, without a global order. Because old votes can still complete a confirmation after a validator change, Sui pauses the fast path at every epoch boundary and drops both payments of an owner who signs two conflicting ones; FastPay and Nano lock it forever. RAI changes validators without pausing. Outgoing validators build each epoch's checkpoint from signed reports while the next epoch already votes; overlap rules keep the two epochs consistent. A handover that does not stop cannot drop a payment that may already be final, so checkpoints keep one side of every double spend, final within two epochs of normal operation. We prove safety and termination and model-check the handover rules. On a wide-area deployment with six validators and 8-second epochs, a prototype keeps confirming through boundaries that stall a paused variant, with 6.7 to 38 times lower 95th-percentile latency.
\end{abstract}
