The bars at the top of the left panels mark when RAI and pause are closing an epoch, from the first validator leaving it to the last one seeing its checkpoint decided. Pause stops confirming during its closes and then catches up in a burst; RAI keeps confirming through longer closes, at a higher latency.}
\label{fig:eval-series}
