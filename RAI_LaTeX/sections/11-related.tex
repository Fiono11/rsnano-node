\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
Guerraoui et al.\ characterize the consensus number of asset transfer~\cite{consensusnumber}. FastPay settles prefunded payments without a global order~\cite{fastpay}; Astro builds asynchronous payments on broadcast and, in its extended version, discusses replica reconfiguration with view-tagged messages and account-log transfer~\cite{astro,dbrb}; Zef adds private coins~\cite{zef}; Nano uses account chains with separate send and receive blocks and open representative voting~\cite{nano}. RAI's difference is the frozen-report evidence that preserves first- and final-vote obligations across a boundary while the next epoch is already voting, and the locks that give a contested position a decision, not a deadlock.

Sui Lutris uses consensus for shared objects and checkpoint sequencing, including end-of-epoch handling of processed certificates~\cite{lutris}; it sequences certificates continuously, so it has a stream to drain, where RAI has none. Cuttlefish broadens the fast path with collective objects and FastUnlock~\cite{cuttlefish}; RAI's recovery of a locked account is narrower and needs no new primitive. Pastro studies permissionless asynchronous asset transfer under a dynamic adversary~\cite{pastro}; dynamic Byzantine reliable broadcast and asynchronous Byzantine reconfiguration address broader changing-participation settings~\cite{dbrb,reconfiguration}. PDCC combines certificate-based payments with membership change and periodic settlement; its installation rule accepts certificates for the installed configuration and assumes correct members common to consecutive configurations~\cite{pdcc}, whereas RAI preserves old-epoch certificates assembled later and needs no members in common.

Agreement with external validity is a standard interface~\cite{externalvalidity}; we instantiate it with a leader-based election in the style of Simplex and Kudzu~\cite{simplex,kudzu}. Authenticated set reconciliation is mature~\cite{iblt,whatsthedifference,rateless}. Model checking small configurations of Byzantine protocols with TLC is common practice~\cite{tla}; here it served as a regression test for the handover rules.
