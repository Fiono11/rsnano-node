\section{Overview}
\label{sec:overview}
Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline} shows one epoch transition. Account voting for epoch $e$ is done by committee $K_e$. At its local boundary a validator stops signing epoch-$e$ first and final votes, freezes its report, and opens epoch $e+1$ for the same accounts. The checkpoint $S_e$ is decided by the outgoing committee from $N-f$ reports, while epoch $e+1$ voting is already under way. The successor committee verifies and installs $S_e$; only then does ordinary epoch-$(e+1)$ work become final. Committees are lagged by two epochs: $S_e$ selects the committee that votes in epoch $e+2$, so the committee of $e+1$ is known throughout the transition.

Three things must hold for this pipeline to be safe. Old-epoch finality that can still be formed must survive in $S_e$: the report rules and the construction of Section~\ref{sec:transitions} guarantee it. New-epoch work must not finalize anything the old epoch could still contradict: the predecessor gate holds ordinary successor work provisional until $S_e$ is known, the overlap exception finalizes early only where old-domain exclusion is proven at every position, and a fast certificate never counts a vote cast before its signer knew $S_e$. The outgoing committee supplies exclusion evidence for fresh blocks by \emph{late notarization}: until it installs $S_e$, each member also notarizes in epoch $e$ the blocks it first-votes in epoch $e+1$, with votes that count toward no finality. And every correct validator must install the same $S_e$: this is where the consensus service enters, and it decides only among candidates that RAI's eligibility predicate accepts.

With $N=6$ and $f=p=1$, notarization and normal finality need four votes, fast finality five first votes, and recovery three reporter first votes. If validators $1,2,3,4$ and a Byzantine validator~6 first-vote a block $B$ but dissemination is delayed, a candidate selecting the reports of $1,2,3,5,6$ still sees $h(B)$ in the voted-but-not-ledger sets $G_1,G_2,G_3$, which meet the recovery threshold, and the construction retains $B$ under a recovery record; the five first votes can later form a fast certificate that finalizes it. Every selected report quorum intersects a normal final-vote quorum in at least $p+1$ correct signers and a fast first-vote quorum in at least $f+p+1$: the two exposure cases of the closure theorem.

RAI fixes the report format, the eligibility of a report selection, the construction, the overlap and installation rules, and the lifecycle of epochs; it does not fix how a validator obtains another reporter's frozen sets, nor how the outgoing committee agrees on one eligible candidate (Section~\ref{sec:services}).
